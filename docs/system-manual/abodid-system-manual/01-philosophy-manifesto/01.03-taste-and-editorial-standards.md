# 01.03 — Taste and Editorial Standards

Status: Current  
Last Updated: 2026-09-18  

---

## 1. The Pop Editorial Aesthetic Ethos

The visual and verbal identity of the ecosystem is governed by **Pop Editorial** standards:

1. **High Contrast & Texture**: Crisp monochrome foundations punctuated by vivid, calculated color accents (electric lime, cobalt, amber). Subtle film-grain overlays add tactile depth without impairing legibility.
2. **Typographic Authority**: Clear division between editorial authority (Satoshi sans-serif), technical data precision (Inconsolata monospace), and occasional literary nuance (system serif).
3. **Intentional Density**: Information is dense, structured, and rewarding to explore. Empty space is used intentionally for pacing, not as generic padding.

---

## 2. Writing and Editorial Rules

Every piece of public and internal writing across the ecosystem adheres to strict editorial guidelines:

| Rule | Requirement | Forbidden Anti-Patterns |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Directness** | State the core point in the first sentence. | "In this article, we will explore the fascinating world of..." |
| **Factual Precision** | Use exact measurements, dates, model names, and metrics. | "High performance", "seamless experience", "cutting-edge". |
| **Active Voice** | Clear subject-verb constructions. | Passive corporate phrasing ("decisions were made"). |
| **No Buzzwords** | Use plain English or precise domain terms. | "Synergy", "game-changer", "revolutionary", "disruptive". |
| **Honest Postmortems** | Document bugs, crashes, and mistakes clearly. | Sweeping errors under the rug or sugarcoating failed tests. |

---

## 3. Curatorial Selectivity

The website is an intentional exhibition, not an uncurated archive:
- Photographs undergo rigorous culling before entering public R2 storage.
- Work projects are published only when they represent substantial craft, measurable impact, or genuine technical novelty.
- Reading Digest articles must pass a two-tier verification check before email dispatch.
