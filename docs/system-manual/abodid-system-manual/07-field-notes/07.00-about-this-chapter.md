## 1. Architectural Purpose of Chapter 07

Chapter `07` is an intentionally open, unfinalized staging ground within the Abodid System Manual.

Its sole architectural purpose is to act as a **permanent buffer** for:
- Emerging technologies and pre-standardized browser APIs
- Speculative interface prototypes and interaction sketches
- Field notes, reading observations, and temporary technical investigations
- Open questions and conceptual inquiries that do not yet belong in the permanent production architecture

## 2. Numbering Stability Invariant

By dedicating Chapter `07` to uncommitted and exploratory concepts, the broader 11-chapter numbering system (`00` through `10`) remains completely immutable across the next 10–15 years of technological evolution.

New paradigms can be investigated here without renumbering core operational specifications.
