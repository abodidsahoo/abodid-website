# Privacy policy draft — Save Opportunity by Abodid & Co.

Draft date: 30 October 2026. Intended public location: https://abodid.com/privacy. Review against actual provider settings and deploy before submission. This draft is not a claim that the policy update is already live.

Save Opportunity connects the existing Opportunity Tracker service to a page or link you deliberately choose to save. We do not monitor ordinary browsing in the background. Contact hello@abodid.com for support or privacy requests.

When you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase authenticate your account. We receive your Google account identifier, primary email, name and profile photo to create and personalise the tracker and associate records with you. We request only basic identity scopes, not Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts or your Google password. Installing the extension does not sign you up for marketing. Product updates available on the website require a separate optional, unchecked consent choice that can be changed in your profile.

When you choose a save action, the extension reads the selected page's URL, title, visible text, relevant structured details, capture time and browser timezone. For a selected link, the server may fetch the linked public page. Our server processes the supplied content through OpenRouter and its selected model provider to identify opportunity details and dates. Use public opportunity pages; do not capture private communications, payment/account portals or other sensitive personal content. AI output may be inaccurate and should be checked against the source.

Supabase stores account records, user-owned saved opportunities, durable job receipts, temporary source material, per-user monthly AI usage counts and short-lived concurrency leases. Vercel hosts our APIs/background processing and may retain ordinary operational request/error metadata. Providers receive only the information needed for their described service; authentication tokens and provider secrets are not sent to opportunity webpages or OpenRouter prompts. The configured AI provider's retention and processing terms also apply. We do not promise zero provider retention or make an unverified claim about model training.

The extension uses local browser extension storage for the PKCE/sign-in session, a minimal account display cache and the latest account-bound capture receipt. This storage is not Chrome storage sync. These local records remain until replaced, signed out or the extension's storage is cleared. Signing out removes the local session; uninstalling clears the extension's local data but does not delete your server-side saved records. Data is encrypted in transit over HTTPS; extension-local storage should not be described as encrypted at rest by this extension.

Saved records remain until you delete them or request account deletion. Temporary capture source is redacted after success; after final failure, automated cleanup is scheduled after 24 hours. Unfinished attachment uploads expire after one hour. Cleanup depends on the deployed processing/maintenance schedule; verify operation before promising a strict time guarantee. Monthly usage counts and short-lived concurrency leases support the disclosed trial allowance and cost/abuse prevention. Account deletion removes user-owned usage/lease records through database cascading rules; operational-provider logs follow those providers' settings.

The packaged extension contains no analytics SDK, advertising tracker or automatic browsing-history collector. The separately opened abodid.com website uses its own disclosed analytics and first-party session records. Google, Supabase, Vercel, OpenRouter and the selected AI provider act in the roles described above. Telegram and voice-note transcription apply only if you separately choose those website/bot integrations, as detailed in the site's policy. We do not sell captured data or use it for advertising, creditworthiness or unrelated profiling.

You can review/edit/delete saved opportunities in your tracker and sign out of the extension. For account deletion, access/export requests or privacy questions, contact hello@abodid.com; a full self-service account-deletion UI is not currently provided. We will update this policy when practices materially change.

Provider policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy ; https://supabase.com/privacy ; https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy ; https://openrouter.ai/privacy . Confirm the configured model provider and its current policy before publishing final copy.
